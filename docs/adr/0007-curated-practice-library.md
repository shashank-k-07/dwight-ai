# Recommendations come from a curated Practice Library, not the LLM's general knowledge

Each Recommendation applies one Practice from Dwight's curated, versioned Practice Library, adapted by the LLM to the Initiative's Sessions and the Customer's Infra Profile. Letting the LLM give advice straight from its general knowledge would have been faster to build. We rejected it because that advice goes stale, can't be cited, and may be made up, and an Admin needs to trace any Recommendation back to a named, reviewable Practice before acting on it. The library also improves over time and is costly for a competitor to reproduce.

## Consequences

The LLM chooses and adapts Practices, but it never calculates the dollar figures. Those come from the Waste findings (ADR 0006).
